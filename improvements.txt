HP Sweep Conclusions
=================================
Ran 55 Optuna TPE trials sweeping pop_size, max_tree_size, num_constants,
coeff_lr, coeff_opt_steps and gamma. Best score: 639.3 (trial 19).

Key findings:
- Large pop_size (128) and large max_tree_size (63) correlate with better scores;
  small populations (16) almost always converge to the degenerate -1391 attractor.
- ~50% of trials collapsed to score -1391.95 regardless of settings. Root causes:
    (a) Exp() overflow produces NaN outputs -> argmax always returns action 0
    (b) "do nothing" is a local fitness optimum vs. random-firing trees (no fuel cost)
    (c) No elitism + noisy fitness (8 episodes) means good solutions are not reliably
        preserved or distinguished from bad ones.
- Best config: pop=128, tree_size=63, constants=2, lr=1.69e-4, opt_steps=250,
  gamma=0.98. These are now the defaults in config.py.
